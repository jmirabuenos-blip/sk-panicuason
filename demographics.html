High Susceptibility

Rain-Induced Landslides

Due to raw sloped terrains sitting along the foothills of Mount Isarog, seasonal heavy typhoons create elevated displacement vulnerabilities across peripheral agricultural settlement segments.

Moderate Risk

Volcanic Lahar & Mudflows

Monitored directly via PHIVOLCS under Mount Isarog's sector frameworks. Heavy precipitation channels surface sediment hazards through historical riverbanks and drainage basins.

Low / Low-Lying Risk

Flash Flooding

While the overall high elevation of 334 meters completely prevents prolonged standard flooding, low-lying drainage catchments experience brief flash occurrences during extreme rain return periods.
