SK Fuel Assist Project

Limited Slots

Who can Apply? ACTIVE delivery riders (food or parcel) who are residents of Barangay Panicuason OR parcel delivery riders who have been assigned in Barangay Panicuason for at least 2 months.

📋 Required Documents Checklist:
  • 01. Driver's License
  • 02. Proof of Delivery Service (Company/App ID)
  • 03. Proof of Residency in Panicuason (Updated Valid ID addressed in Panicuason / Voter's Certificate) OR Proof of Route Assignment (Employer/Head's Certification)

To apply: Submit at the SK Office and look for SK Chairperson John Mark P. Cosa.

TESDA Scholarship Alert!

Technical Courses

Ready to level up your creativity and digital design skills? Apply now for Visual Graphics Design NC III under the Training for Work Scholarship Program (TWSP) at Camarines Sur International School Inc. Offers 501 hours of face-to-face classes at CSIS-Naga (Monday–Saturday | 8:00 AM–5:00 PM).

📋 Requirements:
  • Photocopy of Birth Certificate / Barangay Clearance
  • Photocopy of TOR / Form 138 / Diploma
  • 2 pcs Passport Size Picture & 4 pcs 1x1 Picture
  • Long Brown Envelope
🎯 Eligibility Criteria:
  • Filipino Citizen aged 18 years old & above
  • At least Senior High School Graduate
  • No ongoing TESDA scholarship & not employed
