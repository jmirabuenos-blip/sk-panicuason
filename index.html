Who We Are

Dedicated to the Youth of Our Community

The Sangguniang Kabataan is the official youth council of the barangay, committed to uplifting the lives of every young Filipino through meaningful programs and genuine service.

We believe that the youth are not just the future — they are the present. Our council works tirelessly to create opportunities, amplify youth voices, and foster civic engagement across all sectors of our community.
