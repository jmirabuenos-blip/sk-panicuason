Growth Through the Decades

Panicuason's story is told through the steady growth of its people — from a small mountain community to a recognized barangay of Naga City.

Pre-
1990

Early Settlement

Panicuason sits at approximately 334 meters above sea level on the foothills of Mount Isarog, roughly 5.93 km to its east. The barangay's elevated terrain and proximity to the mountain shaped the way of life of its early settlers — primarily farming and small-scale agriculture. The name "Panicuason" is believed to derive from the local Bikol language, reflecting the natural character of the land.

Origins
1990

First Census Record — 1,236 Residents

The May 1990 Census formally recorded Panicuason's population at 1,236 individuals across 222 households, with an average household size of 5.50 members. This marks the earliest documented demographic baseline of the barangay under modern Philippine statistics.

Census 1990
1995

Slight Dip — 1,366 Residents

By September 1995, the population edged up to 1,366 across 214 households, showing modest growth of 1.89% annually. The slight decrease in the number of households despite population growth suggests household consolidation during this period.

Census 1995
2000

Population Surge — 1,847 Residents

The 2000 Census recorded a significant jump to 1,847 residents — a 6.68% annualized growth rate, the highest in recorded history. The number of households swelled to 332, reflecting increased settlement and family formation within the barangay during the late 1990s.

Census 2000
2007

Continued Growth — 2,246 Residents

The August 2007 Census placed Panicuason's population at 2,246 across 396 households, with a 2.73% annual growth rate. This period marked the barangay crossing the 2,000-resident threshold, signifying its growing importance within Naga City.

Census 2007
2010

Temporary Decline — 2,033 Residents

Unusually, the 2010 Census recorded a decline to 2,033 — a negative growth of -3.56% annually. The number of households remained at 396, suggesting that some residents may have temporarily relocated or migrated during this period, a pattern seen in several upland barangays of Camarines Sur.

Census 2010
2015

Strong Recovery — 2,715 Residents

The 2015 Census saw the strongest recorded recovery, with population jumping to 2,715 across 555 households — a 5.66% annualized growth rate. The median age of 19.64 reflects a very young community, with youth aged 15–19 forming the largest age group at 385 individuals (14.18%). Young dependents aged 14 and below comprised 36.83% of the population.

Census 2015
2020

Present Day — 3,100 Residents

The May 2020 Census confirmed Panicuason's population at 3,100 — the highest in its recorded history, representing a 2.83% annualized growth from 2015. In 30 years, the barangay grew by 1,864 people. Panicuason now accounts for 1.48% of Naga City's total population, 0.15% of Camarines Sur, and 0.051% of the Bicol Region.
