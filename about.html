Our Foundation

Mission & Vision

The principles that guide every program, decision, and action of our council.

Our Mission

To empower the youth of our barangay by providing meaningful programs, accessible opportunities, and a platform for active civic participation. We are committed to nurturing responsible, skilled, and compassionate young leaders who will serve their community with integrity and passion.

Our Vision

A barangay where every young person is heard, valued, and empowered — where youth leadership drives sustainable community development, fosters unity, and creates a future of hope, progress, and opportunity for all residents regardless of background or circumstance.

What We Believe

Our Core Values

Six values that define how we lead, serve, and grow together.

Integrity

We lead with honesty and transparency in every action and decision we make.

Innovation

We seek creative solutions to community challenges through fresh ideas.

Service

We put the needs of our youth and community above personal interests.

Passion

We pour enthusiasm and dedication into every program and initiative we run.

Unity

We celebrate diversity and work together as one team for one community.

Excellence

We hold ourselves to the highest standards in governance and delivery.
