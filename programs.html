Our Programs

Initiatives crafted to empower, educate, and uplift every young person in our barangay.

What We Offer

Programs & Initiatives

Each program is designed with the youth in mind — accessible, impactful, and community-driven.

Educational Assistance Program

Provides scholarships, school supplies, and academic support to deserving youth who face financial barriers. We partner with local schools to identify and assist students in need.

Education Scholarship

Sports & Recreation Program

Organizes inter-barangay leagues, fitness sessions, and sports clinics to promote physical health and teamwork. Open to all youth aged 15 to 30 regardless of skill level.

Sports Health

Livelihood & Skills Training

Free workshops on entrepreneurship, cooking, handicrafts, digital skills, and more. Designed to give out-of-school youth and young adults practical tools for employment.

Livelihood Skills

Youth Health & Wellness

Free medical and dental missions, mental health awareness sessions, and health seminars to ensure every young person has access to basic healthcare services.

Health Wellness

Arts & Culture Program

Celebrates local talent through festivals, art exhibits, cultural performances, and creative workshops that preserve heritage and nurture artistic expression among youth.

Arts Culture

Environmental Awareness

Tree planting drives, clean-up campaigns, and eco-education seminars that teach youth to be stewards of the environment and take action against climate change locally.

Environment Advocacy

Digital Literacy Program

Basic computer training, internet safety, and digital skills workshops to bridge the digital divide and equip youth with 21st-century competencies for the modern workforce.

Technology Digital

Leadership & Governance

Youth leadership camps, seminars on civic responsibility, and governance training to prepare the next generation of community leaders and public servants.

Leadership Governance

Community Service & Outreach

Feeding programs, disaster response volunteers, and community clean-up drives that instill the value of bayanihan and genuine service to those most in need.

Community Outreach
